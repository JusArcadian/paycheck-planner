# Prompt Pack (works in ChatGPT or Claude)

Copy a prompt, fill in the [brackets], paste it into ChatGPT or Claude.
Paste in `config/listing.json` when a prompt says "my product details".
**Never paste in your `.env` file, API keys, or buyers' emails.**

---

## 1. Listing & SEO

**Fresh Etsy titles**
> Here are my product details: [paste listing.json]. Write 5 Etsy titles under 140 characters. Put the most-searched phrase first. No ALL CAPS, no emoji, no promises about taxes or saving money.

**Tag audit**
> These are my 13 Etsy tags: [paste]. For each, tell me whether a real buyer would type it, and suggest a better one if not. Each tag must be 20 characters or less.

**Turn features into benefits**
> Rewrite each feature as a benefit a 19-year-old with their first job would care about, in under 15 words each: [paste features].

## 2. Marketing content

**TikTok / Reels hooks**
> Give me 10 hooks (first 2 seconds of a video) for a budget spreadsheet aimed at students and first-jobbers. The angle is "your paycheck is smaller than you think." Make them conversational, not salesy.

**30-day posting plan**
> Make a 30-day short-video plan to promote [product name]. Mix: 40% useful money tips, 30% showing the spreadsheet, 20% relatable humor, 10% direct "link in bio". One line per day.

**Pinterest pins**
> Write 10 Pinterest pin titles and descriptions (under 100 words each) for a paycheck budget template. Include natural keywords like "budget template" and "50/30/20".

## 3. Customer support

**Reply to a buyer**
> A buyer wrote: "[paste message]". Draft a friendly, short reply. My product: Google Sheets + Excel budget template. The Sheets link is in their receipt PDF. Don't promise refunds unless I say so.

**Build an FAQ from messages**
> Here are 10 buyer questions I got: [paste, with names removed]. Group them into themes and write an FAQ section I can add to my listing.

## 4. New product ideas

**Find the next niche**
> My budget template for first-jobbers sells on Etsy. Suggest 10 spin-off templates for specific groups (e.g., nursing students, gig drivers, new parents). For each: who it's for, the specific money problem, and 3 must-have features.

**Bundle ideas**
> Suggest 3 bundles I could sell with my budget template (e.g., debt payoff tracker + savings goals). Give each a name, a price idea, and why buyers would want it.

## 5. Coding help (for the scripts in this kit)

**Explain an error**
> I'm a beginner. I ran `[command]` and got this error: [paste full error]. Explain what it means like I'm 5, then tell me step by step how to fix it. This is the file: [paste the .py file].

**Add a feature**
> Here is my build_template.py: [paste]. I want to add [feature]. Show me only the lines to change, where they go, and explain each one in plain English.
